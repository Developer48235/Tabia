### Nature of the Dragons

Ancient, magical guardians protecting the realm, whose origins predate mortal recorded history.

- The Emperors once sought their counsel, but the dragons have grown distant for unexplained reasons.
- Most dragons no longer view mortals as sentient beings, regarding them instead as mere automatons in a mechanistic universal structure.
- A number of dragons are unnamed because they chose not to speak to mortals, so their names are only known among their own kind.

## Ghosts

Ectoplasm: Goop. If the ghost touches something it can leave behind ectoplasm.
- What is it touching? Some can touch things with Telekinesis, therefore do not leave behind ectoplasm. Poltergeists throw things about (or at you).
	- If it touches A and B, you get a clue on its movement mode.
	- If its only touching doors but not floors, you know it is floating.
	- How many doors did it touch in order to find you?
	- Some ghosts can find you if you're hiding. Some cannot.
- Can you see its spirit orbs?
- Can you hear its ghost whispers?
	- Provoke it by saying its name?
	- Does it talk back?
- The salt test. Will it cross over salt? A demon cannot cross salt.
- What is its territory?
- Where is it going?

Africa Rules
- Large animals can be seen a kilometer away.
- Very few trees prevent ambush predators. So child apex predators struggle to eat.
- Everything acts in pacts, even Hippos. Crocodiles sit around egg guarding.
- Nothing stays private, especially kills. When you kill, vultures and hyenas quickly arrive.  Let others do the hunting, then steal their kills.
- Water hole ambushes.
- Warthogs don't run fast.
- Leapards. A permanent lesson to check trees before you sleep. 
- Month 5 water holes shrink with the dry season. Birds migrate.
- Avoid mid day heat.
- Fly swarms go for wounds.
- Animals send scouts first to water holes. Buffalo post sentries and run at top speed without hesitation. Elephants find new migration routes when you enter an area. Lions eat quickly and eat the good bits when you're not around. Lions start stalking you to rob you.

Commitment Hunter: One that upon contact decides to either attack or leave. They dont wait around studying, because they are always hungry.

Noncommitment Hunter: Example is Lions. They look for weaknesses in your armour. They study your attention span. They figure out when you're sleeping. They figure out the reach of your weapons. They watch for signs that you are startled. They approach, not too near but not too far just beyond your range and test you.

The ancient logic of predator and pray: size, speed, territory.

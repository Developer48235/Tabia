# People

NPCs — allies, rivals, quest-givers, and anyone else the party might meet.

Political plots and schemes are iconic elements. Whether true or not, everyone thinks everything done that is cool is caused by Spy Masters. In the in-game lore, the schemers are given the credit for everything.
## Minor Noble Houses
They serve two purposes, providing potential allies who can join your side, providing a backdrop of conflict and plot twists.
- A sickly family of frail build that consistently chooses the wrong side in a war. They are in proximity to the Imperial Lands, and so are regularly punished. They often switch sides.
- The head of the household is a child whose main trait is that he is about to die at all times. Claim to be the purest descendants of the Iron People.
- Claim to be descendants a legendary King, they are a secondary house prone to fervant beliefs. They will switch religion if it favours them.
- Embodiments of cruelty, their scion lords invariably bounce between Lawful Evil or Chaotic Evil. They typically do not make excuses for their villainy, they either just enjoy it or see it as practical necessity.
- A massive family of petty villains. Nobody respectable would marry with them. Their house name is a synonym for evil, despite them not always being this way.
- A family of villains that engage in aggressive nepotism. Their claim to infamy is that they got away with killing an Emperor, which they did in a pretty funny manner using a large rock. Over time they lost most of their castles.
- Two houses that have an ancestral family feud that dates back further than anyone could possibly remember. They are in a cycle of vengeance. They no qualms against war crimes.

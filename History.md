## Information
Characters often act on false information. To understand why this important requires understanding why information is important in the first place.

Important Information
- **Reliability** Who are reliable allies?
- **Responsibility**. Blaming people for things they didn't do. Assuming people have clean hands when they do not. Not realising that you will get responsibility for crimes committed by others.
- **Misunderstanding how others feel**. Thinking everyone feels admiration for your father. Thinking two regions won't ally because they hate each other.
- **Finding "evidence" of conspiracies** Such as foreign coins.
- **Mismanagement of money**. Spending money on ships or festivals when it is better spent paying your debts.
- **Thinks a social problem is someone else's problem**. Such as thinking that fanatics will go after the ''greatest evil'' rather than the evil they can most easily confront.
- **Thinking people are dead when they're alive**. Likewise thinking people are alive when they are dead.
- **Naivety**. Thinking people lack an agenda when they do.

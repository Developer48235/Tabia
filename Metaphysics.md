# Metaphysics

[← Back to Tabia home](./README.md)

See also: [World](./World.md) — Metaphysics, Creation & Religion.

- **Vital essence** — sometimes called Life, Heart, Faith, or Instinct, it is the fundamental energies that animate reality and fuel its beings' instinctual behaviours.
- **Vitae** — creatures defined by their vital essence.
- **Quintessence** — aligned metaphysical essence from which souls are formed.
- **Maelstrom** — vast swathes of uncharted, unclaimed terrain that metaphysically border the planes.
- **Soul** — the essential metaphysical life energy of a living creature.
- **Outsider** — one belonging to a different planet or plane from their locality. All outsiders are native to the plane of their creation.
- **Potentiality** — raw, unaligned metaphysical force of the Maelstrom that may eventually become a soul.
- **Mortal** — experiences mortality, wherein the creature's life has a definite beginning and ending linked to the points in time when its soul is joined to and released from a vessel.
- **Dark Tapestry** — the darkness between the stars where people believe true madness lies. The vast, cold void of space is too alien for even the gods to comprehend.
- **Celestial Body** — an object or cohesive group of objects demonstrated to exist in the observable Universe.
- **Portal** — a gateway that directly links one location to another, often by magical or supernatural means. All but one celestial body in the solar system are linked by portals.
- **Planar Frequency** — each plane's essence exhibits a distinctly repeating pattern. This can be used to recognize and disrupt certain phenomena, such as planar rifts.
- **Basrakal** — a settlement that defies all logic, located on stable islands of reality within the ever-changing Maelstrom.
- **Plane** — a vast dimension with various physical traits, such as gravity and chronology, and metaphysical traits, such as alignment and quintessential composition.
- **Demiplane** — each plane is effectively a standalone, infinite universe, differentiating them from demiplanes, which are finite and measurable.
- **-Scapes** — unusual dimensions that coexist within a plane, which can have different physical properties.
- **Extradimensional / Nondimensional** — describes a metaphysical existence outside of any plane or dimension.
- **Dimension of Time** — a hidden, poorly known, almost unreachable dimension of time that touches all known planes except timeless ones.
- **Philosophy** — the study of general and fundamental problems, such as those connected with existence, knowledge, values, reason, mind, and language. Philosophies differ from religions in that they are teachings and ways of thinking propounded by a mortal founder who, usually, does not become a deity.
- **Occultism** — a philosophy that believes in incomprehensible hidden forces beneath and between the perceptible realities of the planes.

# Languages

[← Back to Cultures](./README.md)

See also: [Naming](../World/Naming.md) — languages should be given names that make it easy to remember what they're about as a concept.

- ❌ There are no racial languages. Most mortal races have been intermingling for so long that language is based on geography.
- ❌ There are no more imperial languages.
- ✅ Some languages are fluid dialect continuums, others are manufactured and manipulated.

## Languages

- **Amalgam** — the language of the folk of the Commons, more like a multicultural collection of languages and a dialect continuum. It is deeply immersed in ancient agricultural culture, so even the Treants & Fey know of it. The language of nature sciences and ancient folklore. It is spoken by all of the ancestries in the Commons, and owned by none of them.
- **Axiomatic** — the language of interplanar trade. It is also the language of engineering & music notation. Dwarves used to use a similar language but voluntarily moved to this one.
- **Darkspeech** — its utterance and reading it causes corruption. It was invented by the Dark Master, the first corrupted mortal, to corrupt more of his kind.
- **Metamorph** — the cosmopolitan language of the local ruling class, which originated from shapeshifting interplanar infiltrators. The history of the language is the history of regime change. It is the language of Contract Law, through Devil influence. New words can be dynamically constructed from old words.
- **Foundation** — the language of the foreign ruling class of the Orthodoxy, which was influenced by religion and philosophy. Its writing is an art form, and most Orthodoxy citizens are illiterate. Their merchants are the largest trading partner of the Commons.
- **Nomadic** — the dialect continuum of the Northern plains tribes. Those who speak Nomadic can understand some Northern Amalgam, but not the other way around.
- **Zircon** — the dialect continuum of the Southern water tribes. Those who speak Zircon can understand some Southern Amalgam, but not the other way around. Zircon is named after the Water Dragon who fell in love with a mythical hero called the Avatar, so she created this language to speak to the mortals there.
- **Corsair** — the language of foreign pirates. It is a standard dialect of Zircon.
- **Thieves' Cant** — secret language of thieves and revolutionaries, invented by local bards. Perform can be used to encode longer messages. It uses other languages and slang terms. Its written form is of signs and graffiti.
- **Ranger Sign** — runic symbols, more like a secret coding system. Locals may recognise some Ranger Sign if it is used regularly.
- **Druidic** — _not yet written._
- **Hieroglyphic** — the last Human imperial language, now a dead language of the Bronze Age, mainly used by the Undead.
- **Terran** — the language of Dragons, and the first language of Earth.
- **Grotte** — the language of the Underdark.

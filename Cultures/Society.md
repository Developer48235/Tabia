## Mortals

- ✅ Non-human ancestries are not merely cosmetic changes. Their existence influenced societal development.
- ❌ There are no mono-ancestry states, like ethnostates.
- ✅ Society adapted to deal with local threats. This can be seen in the norms, behaviours, architecture, punishment, and politics.

### Apex Predators

In an environment with potentially magical apex predators, Humans did not pastorally sprawl across the Commons. They evolved sensitivity to ambient magical fields. Humans do not fear magic.

### Sentinel Sleep Pattern

Mortal sleep cycles mirror polyphasic "sentinel sleep" — never having the entire tribe unconscious at once. There is no standard 9-6 job.

### Wanderer

This word evolved to have connotations for those who have a death wish. Strangers who enter your land were not seen as threats. Ostracization is the ultimate capital punishment, hence Etiquette culture.

### Settlements

Settlements are half underground, in order to provide haven from aerial predators. They rely on Chain Harpoon Ballistae to ground flying targets. Local monster biology became a state secret, as it served a defensive role against mortal invaders.

### Clear Zones

Outside fortified settlements, land would be cleared for kilometers. No forests or dense scrub would be tolerated near settlements, to prevent stalkers from using cover. Farming would rely heavily on terrace gardens inside fortress perimeters, rooftop hydro-plots, or fortified concentric baileys defended by continuous watchtowers.

### Egg Raiding as a Duty

Statecraft would organize seasonal, state-sponsored culling expeditions to destroy monster nests, steal unhatched clutches, and eliminate broods before they reached adulthood.

### Building Tall

Due to Dwarves, towers are anchored into deep granite bedrock using pre-stressed stone tiebacks and interlocking keystone arches, rather than relying solely on mortar.

Massive cantilever rings: high-altitude residential and commercial platforms thrust outward from a central "spine" tower via reinforced cantilever trusses. This leaves the lower perimeter sheer and unclimbable, while expanding living space higher up.

Dragon Knights provide high-heat smelters and lightweight, high-tensile metallurgical alloys, creating structural steel frames centuries ahead of our historical timeline.

### Demonic Alliances

Evil mortals would form pacts with demons. Geopolitics and strife caused some desperate places to entertain the nightmares of the Abyss.

### Feudalism vs. Statism

Certain societies embraced greater feudalism, i.e. a hierarchy based on hereditary rule and military obligation. This made them more decentralised but less capable of fielding larger armies, and also led to a lack of rule of law and external factionalism, as there are many distinct hierarchies set up into constant violent political conflict.

Certain societies embraced statism, which allows them to have a strong central government capable of raising large armies, but mires them in constant internal factionalism and bureaucracy.

## Kinship & Demographics

### The Soulless Condition

Soullessness and mutations occur sporadically. A large percentage of the general population is soulless — an open secret.

### Status of the Soulless

The soulless cannot be resurrected with raise dead, and many rulers do not consider them to have moral value. A peasant revolution among the soulless is currently brewing. Historically, those soulless who social climbed were excluded from martial and magical pursuits.

### Kinship Dynamics

Because of soullessness, blood lineages dissolve and parental investment is neglected. Society relies heavily on fictive kin (adoption and "family of the sword") as well as collateral kin (aunts, uncles, and cousins).

### Descent & Power

Operates through ambilineal descent, where power determines the descent group. Because power is perceived in multiple ways, varied descent lines emerge, with both women and men rising as great powers or scholars.

### Tournaments & "Hidden Champions"

Tournaments use promises of wealth to draw out capable figures. However, individuals who are paranoid or indifferent to wealth, prizes, and ego remain "hidden champions" — occupying places or situations that harbor profound, untapped talent.

## The Squire System & Warrior Culture

A major, ignored source of conflict across the realm.

- Most squires cannot afford knighthood and are rarely trained effectively; they are exploited as free labor in exchange for adventure and cultural immersion (which reproduces local warrior traditions).
- Unknighted squires typically end up as common bandits or men-at-arms. Their experience as squires who lived on the road made them well suited for this.
- Ideas of "Honour, Idealism, and Virtue" serve as state propaganda to control the poor. The lower classes leverage these ideals to project loyalty and climb the social ladder, though genuine believers exist.
- This moral framework masks the violent reality of social advancement, which is driven by feuds, bloody vendettas, and realpolitik.

## Martial Artists

Martial artists take a lot of time and effort to produce. Some techniques become too good — too dangerous — so they get banned to ensure the social reproduction of martial artists. For sports martial arts, they are banned for competition. Military martial arts are only banned if they are too dangerous to use and learn.

This produced four basic kinds:

- Dancing turned to Sport Wrestling
- Military Wrestling
- Sword Fencing
- Organised Strikes

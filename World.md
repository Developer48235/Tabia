The pitch for the setting: tone, genre touchstones, cosmology, the calendar, core conceits that make Tabia distinct from a generic fantasy world.

_Not yet written._

## Naming

### Rules

- **Planetary Dragons** are named after geology concepts, such as epochs. Their naming, type, and family reflects the formation of the Earth and Moon.

### Guide

- For animals, use compound names.
- Languages and major cities should be given names that make it easy to remember what they're about as a concept.
- Avoid unnecessary proper nouns.
- Avoid too many names from one culture, to avoid problems like Eurocentrism.

## Cosmology

### The Moon

Orbits at half the distance, so appears twice as large.

## Metaphysics, Creation & Religion

See also: [Bestiary](./Bestiary.md) for the Dragons, [Factions](./Factions.md) for Religion.

### Creation of Mortals

Dragons did not create the mortal races. Mortals were brought in by another group entirely to serve as cannon fodder against the Dark Pantheon.

### The Dark Pantheon & Devils

Mysterious forces and interplanar rivals to the Dragons; the devils originate directly from this pantheon.

### Ancient Rocks

Humans gained their knowledge and power by studying ancient rocks.

### Foreign Gods

All gods and pantheons are foreign to this realm, and those in positions of power generally care little about them.

### Coastal Magic

Coastal populations acquired their magical secrets from foreign lands across the sea.

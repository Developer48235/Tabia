The player characters, their composition, and their history.

Party Premise

- Ten years ago a crack team was sent to prison by a military court for a crime they did not commit. They promptly escaped from the maximum security stockade, to the Underground. Today still wanted by the government they survive as soldiers as fortune. If you have a problem and no one else can help and if you can find them may you can hire them.

Missions
- B1
- B2 - Hub and Spoke Settlement with Adventure Site
- Indiana Jones
- Heist
- X1 - Wilderness Exploration Hex Crawl
- Red Hand of Doom - Epic ticking clock war campaign
- Sunless Citadel - Ruleset introduction - explore a sunken fortress torn apart by a turf war between kobolds and goblins
- Shackled City - gang war leading to demonic incursion
- Curse of the Crimson Throne - urban campaign with political intrigue, revolution, and a deeply memorable villain. Dark fantasy city in revolt; V for Vendetta meets D&D. The entire campaign is centered around the city of Korvosa. The King dies under mysterious circumstances, leaving his young, tyrannical Queen in charge. The players must navigate riots, a city-wide plague, martial law, and political assassinations to save their home. The villain is active, cruel, and deeply satisfying to finally defeat.
- Rise of the Runelords - It starts with a simple goblin raid on a frontier town festival and slowly scales up into a battle against an ancient, awakened wizard-king.
- Season of Ghosts - Spirited Away meets survival horror mystery. The town becomes completely cut off from the outside world by a supernatural curse. The campaign is structured around the four seasons of a single year, forcing players to protect the townsfolk, manage resources, and solve a massive, time-bending mystery to escape.
